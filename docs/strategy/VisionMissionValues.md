# Vision, Mission and Values

Written 2026-11-24. Detective Sudoku (codename ruby) is a Jini Solutions game.
The requirements derived from this page are the `STRAT-MIS-*` and `STRAT-VAL-*`
files in [requirements/strategy_requirements](../requirements/strategy_requirements/).

## Vision

**The clearest and fairest detective logic puzzle on a phone**: the one a
player trusts, because when they get a case wrong the mistake is in their
reasoning and never in the wording, the art or the app.

The genre is young and growing fast (see [MarketResearch.md](MarketResearch.md)).
Its leading apps are thin, and their reviews repeat the same complaints: clues
that can be read two ways, ads that interrupt, and notes that cannot record what
the player has worked out. That is the gap this game is built for.

## Mission

**To give every player a steady supply of small, honest whodunits, free, that
can always be solved by thinking.**

We do this by:

- **Certifying every case by machine.** A case ships only when a solver has
  proved it has exactly one solution and can be solved without guessing.
- **Giving every clue one exact meaning**, and showing that meaning on the grid
  when the player asks.
- **Using simple words**: places, rooms and objects a child could name.
- **Keeping the money honest**: one banner, and a rewarded video the player
  chooses to watch for hint coins. Nothing else in version 1.
- **Building with discipline**: the rules live in a pure engine, the generator
  is deterministic, and every rule in the docs carries its reason and date.

## Values

**V1. Clarity before cleverness.** A clue the player can misread is a defect,
however elegant the puzzle. *Why:* ambiguous wording is the best-evidenced
complaint in the genre (PainPointsAndConcepts.md, P1).

**V2. Fair play.** One solution, no guessing, and a difficulty label that means
something. *Why:* a logic puzzle that needs a guess breaks its one promise.

**V3. Respect for the player's time and attention.** No ads during a case, no
energy, no keys, no rating prompt on the first solve, no sound taken over by an
ad. *Why:* 43% of puzzle players say they quit over too many ads
(TargetAudience.md).

**V4. Honesty.** The store listing, the hint prices and the ad exchange say
what is true. An ad is offered as a trade the player can read before agreeing.

**V5. Our own work.** The genre's mechanics are common ground; another
author's puzzles, names, text and art are not. Every case is generated by our
generator. *Why:* the genre has a living author and publisher, and copying them
would be wrong as well as risky (RiskAnalysis.md, R2).

**V6. Simple and readable.** Simple setting names, readable pixel icons,
letters on tokens. A square's meaning must be visible at phone size.

**V7. Written down.** A decision that is not in the docs with its reason and
date will be made again, differently.
