# Market Research

Researched 2026-11-24 from the twelve Google Play listings named by the owner
(fetched with `hl=en&gl=US`), one screenshot per app, the three reviews Play
shows per listing, and press coverage. **Limits:** no more than three reviews
per app were readable, only one screenshot each was examined, and most iOS
pages were not retrieved. Every figure is from the listing on that date unless
a source is named. Store numbers move; treat this page as a dated snapshot.

## 1. Where the genre comes from

The genre was set by the *Murdoku* puzzle books by Manuel Garand, a Montreal
puzzle maker ([Hachette](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/manuel-garand/murdoku/9781454961796/)).

- **Books:** the first volume (80 puzzles, Puzzlewright, 28 Oct 2025), then
  *Back in Time*, *Around the World* (US 27 Oct 2026) and *Beyond the Grave*
  (listed for 4 May 2027)
  ([Penguin AU](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/murdoku-volume-2-back-in-time-9781405995887),
  [Hachette](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/manuel-garand/murdoku-beyond-the-grave/9781454968122/)).
- **Rules in one paragraph:** people are placed on a grid of named areas, one
  per row and column; some objects can be stood on and some block; clues say
  where each suspect was; the victim takes the last square; whoever was alone
  with the victim is the murderer
  ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/murdoku-explained-how-the-murder-mystery-sudoku-puzzle-works/)).
- **Popularity:** 140,000+ copies sold in Spain
  ([Euronews, Sep 2026](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/01/murdoku-and-the-adult-activity-book-craze-the-trend-sweeping-spain));
  sold out and reprinted in the Netherlands after a TikTok wave
  ([JFK](https://jfk.nl/news/murdoku-puzzelboek-hype-2026-kopen-bestellen-reserveren/));
  made viral in Spain by a streamer
  ([Hipertextual](https://hipertextual.com/actualidad/me-he-vuelto-adicto-a-los-murdokus-y-ahora-he-encontrado-el-juego-perfecto-para-el-movil-hay-miles-y-son-100-gratis/)).
  The publisher claims "more than 1 million copies in print" and 15 languages;
  **that figure is the publisher's own and was not independently confirmed.**
- **No official app exists.** There is a free web version with new cases on
  the author's site and printable sheets. Every app below is an independent
  take on the genre.
- *Murdle* (G.T. Karber) is a different series with narrative cross-reference
  clues and no placement grid.

What this means for us: the audience was created by a book, arrived on phones
in mid-2026, and is served by apps that are months old. See also
[RiskAnalysis.md](RiskAnalysis.md) R1 and R2 on the name and on originality.

## 2. The twelve competitors

### 2.1 Enigmic: Crime Puzzle Cases — Infinity Games (`com.tangramgamestudio.enigmic`)
The market leader: 500K+ installs, 4.4★ from 13.5K reviews, updated 6 Nov
2026. Ads plus purchases; rated Everyone. Clean cream flat UI; tools Cross /
Place / Erase / Hint; one solution per case; a recent "object catalog".
Reviews: asks for a rating after every puzzle "including the first"; the
most-upvoted review attacks the wording "right column" ("that means five
different columns… If you mean the FAR RIGHT column, use that wording!!!");
"App mutes phone during ads"; a fan asks for suspect-specific crosses and for
case intros. Press praised it as all free with ads only after a level and ad
removal at €4.99 (Hipertextual, above); an aggregator quotes iOS users on an ad
after every puzzle ([game-solver](https://game-solver.com/enigmic-crime-puzzle-cases/)).

### 2.2 Whodoku – Murder sudoku — ES GAMES (`app.whodoku.game`)
50K+ installs, 4.4★ from 1.17K reviews, updated 14 Oct 2026. City case packs,
a case-file paper look, realistic floor plans, an Observe → Deduce → Accuse
flow. Reviews: praised as the only app with furniture "drawn from the right
perspective" but docked a star for AI-generated images; realistic images "make
it hard to see clues"; marking controls "cumbersome"; playable ads that
"you cannot exit".

### 2.3 Sudoku Detective: Murder Case — Trasco studio (`com.trasco.sudoku.detective.murder.mystery.logic.puzzle`)
50K+ installs, 4.3★ from 1.05K reviews, updated 6 Oct 2026. Ornate dark look
with painted portraits; cases gated by daily keys (buy, or watch a video).
Reviews: ads "NOT invasive" and fast developer replies; but answers
"previously marked correct are now wrong" on replay. Its last update promises
"clues and texts are clearer". Spanish strings show in English screenshots.

### 2.4 Sudoku Logic: Detective Case — Wordloco (`com.wordloco.detectivegrid`)
50K+ installs, no public rating, updated 20 Nov 2026. 6×6 and 9×9, three
difficulties, procedurally generated, notes and auto-notes, offline. Dark UI
with emoji-style icons. No reviews visible.

### 2.5 Murder Sudoku GridNoir — Racorway (`com.racorway.murdersudoku`)
10K+ installs, 4.4★ from 425 reviews, updated 21 Nov 2026. A solo developer.
Claims 1500+ cases (a screenshot says 3000+), themed casebooks, daily
challenge, Confirm / Likely / Discard marks, "optional rewarded ads for extra
hints or early case access", a 24-day December event. Reviews: "Directions get
more complex by the level"; a one-line "AI art".

### 2.6 Caseoku: Murder Mystery Sudoku — CatLaboratory (`com.catlaboratory.crimoku`)
10K+ installs, **3.3★** from 60 reviews, updated 23 Nov 2026. Three shared
daily cases (6×6, 7×7, 8×8), scenes House / Street / Beach / Forest / Office /
Hotel, an "(i)" on each clue with an example. Reviews: "Someone is in row 3 but
the rows and columns aren't numbered so you don't know which way to start
counting… ads take so long"; others praise the difficulty labels as honest.

### 2.7 Krimdoku – Murder Sudoku — Apo-Games (`com.apogames.murdoku`)
10K+ installs, no rating shown, updated 22 Nov 2026. No ads or purchases
listed. The richest tool set: 4×4 to 12×12, a level editor with a uniqueness
checker, community cases, an endless generator, PDF export, a hint giver that
explains the step, and suspect traits (beard, glasses, hair) that clues use.
Credits the book's author as a "fan version".

### 2.8 Cluedoku: Detective Sudoku — Aboba game company (`com.maksimillian.cluedoku`)
1K+ installs, no rating, updated 15 Oct 2026. **Its subtitle is our name**
(RiskAnalysis.md R1). Daily case with streaks, a campaign that introduces one
clue type at a time, grids to 12×12, "smart hints show the next logical step",
ranked duels and co-op, 20 languages, and a Plus subscription that removes ads
and unlocks the hardest tiers.

### 2.9 Crime Sudoku: Murder Mystery — TLL Studios (`com.tlldevelopers.redthread`)
500+ installs, updated 22 Nov 2026. Discloses its ads plainly: "a banner and
an interstitial when you leave a result. Rewarded videos are optional: watch
one for a hint or to double your coins." Two modes, A–D / 1–4 coordinates on
the grid, three-level guided hints with a "Place Tilda there" button, story
casefiles, 27 languages.

### 2.10 Sudoku Murder: Cold Cases — Nikolay Strikhar (`com.leprechaun.murdersudoku`)
10+ installs, updated 10 Nov 2026. **No ads**; a subscription or lifetime
pass. Pixel-art scenes; four tiers "graded by the technique a case needs";
every case solver-checked ("the mistake is in your logic and never in the
wording"); notes per suspect; a Share result button. The closest to our own
concept, and brand new.

### 2.11 Mystery Case: Murder Sudoku — HP CRAFT (`com.hpcraft33.murdermysterysudoku`)
10+ installs, updated 23 Nov 2026, rated Everyone 10+ (Fantasy Violence). Four
themed worlds, warm cartoon cut-away art, up to three stars for no mistakes.

### 2.12 Murder Sudoku: Detective Logic — Inowios (`com.murdersudoku.detective`)
Just launched, updated 22 Nov 2026, rated Everyone 10+ (Fantasy Violence,
Tobacco, Alcohol Reference). Art Deco look, A–G / 1–7 coordinates, a hint card
that explains and offers "Place".

Reading the table: one app has most of the installs with the fewest features;
the feature-rich apps are all tiny and weeks old; nobody combines certified
fairness, exact wording, good notes and a gentle ad model **and** has an
audience yet. Content ratings are "Everyone" for most, "Everyone 10+" for the
two that declare violence.

## 5. Related puzzles worth borrowing from

| Puzzle | Idea that fits a one-per-row-and-column area grid |
|---|---|
| Star Battle / Queens ([rules](https://mspiegel.github.io/queens-without-backtracking/QUEENS.html)) | "Nobody touched, even diagonally" as a scene rule; "exactly one person per area" |
| Clues by Sam ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clues_By_Sam)) | Counting clues ("exactly 2 people in the Kitchen", "more upstairs than downstairs"); testimony revealed as you solve; role tags |
| Zebra / Einstein grids | "Immediately" versus "somewhere" to one side; attributes; conditionals |
| Killer sudoku | Sums or parity over an area |
| Tents | Each person tied to their own object |
| Battleship | Margin counts; squares revealed at the start |
| Others | Line of sight stopped by walls; step distance; exactly one false statement; two time slots |

Which of these the engine carries in v1, and which wait, is decided in
[GameRules.md](../GameRules.md).
