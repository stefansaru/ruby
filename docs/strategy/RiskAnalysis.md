# Risk Analysis

Written 2026-11-24. Likelihood and impact are the agent's judgement (L low, M
medium, H high) and are there to order the list, not to be precise. Risks are
numbered and never renumbered.

| Id | Risk | L | I | Mitigation | Carried by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Name clash.** "Detective Sudoku" is already the subtitle of Cluedoku on Google Play, and "sudoku", "detective" and "murder" are in most competitors' titles. The name may be refused, confused, or impossible to protect. | H | M | Keep the name as the working title (owner decision 2026-11-24). Before the listing is created: search Play and the App Store for the exact title, check trademark registers, and decide. The codename `ruby` and package `com.jinisolutions.ruby` do not depend on the outcome. | STRAT-RSK-001 |
| R2 | **Originality and the book's trademark.** The genre has a living author and publisher. "Murdoku" is their name. Copying puzzles, character names, text or art would be wrong and actionable. | M | H | Mechanics are reused; expression is not. All cases come from our generator; test fixtures are our own; the word "Murdoku" appears in no code, asset or listing copy (it appears in research docs only, as the name of the source). Photos of the book stay in the ignored `input/` folder. | STRAT-VAL-003, PROC-CNT-002 |
| R3 | **AI-art backlash.** Two competitors already lose stars for art that looks generated. Our sheets are generated from prompts, so we are exposed to the same review. | M | M | A single consistent pixel style at low resolution, where the hand of the tool shows least; icon-like objects; no painted portraits; every sheet reviewed by the owner before it is accepted; say nothing false about how the art is made. | STRAT-RSK-002, APP-AST-001 |
| R4 | **Content rating for a murder theme.** A murder theme can raise the rating above "Everyone" and collides with child-directed rules if children are in the audience. With no ads (owner ruling 2026-11-25) the ad-inventory and ad-consent side of this risk is gone; the rating and the audience declaration remain. | M | M | Bloodless by rule: no blood, weapons in use, bodies or gore in art or text. Fill in the rating questionnaire truthfully and record the result. Declare the target audience as not primarily children; settle the age approach before the purchase is built (a purchase by a child is a store-policy matter). A cat mode exists as a wording switch for players who avoid the theme (docs/features/Modes.md). | STRAT-RSK-003, APP-PRV-003 |
| R5 | **Generator quality.** Generated cases can be valid and still dull, repetitive, or oddly worded. | M | H | Ordered gates including clue variety and no redundant clue; difficulty by technique; pinned seeds so a pack can be reviewed and regenerated; a manual read of a sample from every pack before release. | GEN register, PROC-CNT-001 |
| R6 | **A wording or logic defect ships.** One ambiguous clue undoes the product's main claim. | M | H | Wording only from templates tied to the clue type; each clue type has a fixture test; shipped cases replayed by tests; the clue preview lets a player check the meaning themselves. | APP-UX-003, APP-QAL-002 |
| R7 | **Solo capacity.** Scope is large: engine, generator, UI, art, the purchase, privacy, release. | H | M | Rounds with a fixed scope (round 1, 2026-11-24: docs, engine, generator; since then: the playable draft); TODO.md holds everything else; borrow working patterns from amethyst, pushy and day_ok instead of inventing. | PROC-DOC-001 |
| R8 | **A crowded, fast-moving market.** Twelve apps in a few months, several updated the week of this research; the leader has 500K+ installs. | H | M | Compete on the open tier (clarity and fairness), not on volume or social features. Recheck the market before launch. | STRAT-MKT-001 |
| R9 | **The wave passes.** Interest was created by a book and social video and may fade. | M | M | The game stands as a logic puzzle without the trend; the engine's rule variety gives it a longer life than a single format. | STRAT-MKT-001 |
| R10 | **Retired 2026-11-25: ad SDK and consent mistakes.** The game carries no ads and no ad SDK (owner ruling 2026-11-25), so this risk no longer exists. Replaced by R14. | – | – | – | – |
| R11 | **Performance on device.** A solver that is fine on a desktop can stall a phone when it computes a hint on a 9×9 case. | M | M | Measure hint time in the engine benchmarks; set a budget before the hint UI is built. | APP-QAL-004 |
| R12 | **An official app appears.** The author has none today. | M | M | Nothing to do but be good, and keep R2 clean so that an official release changes our competition, not our legality. | STRAT-VAL-003 |
| R13 | **A paid unlock converts few players.** Added 2026-11-25. The game has no ads, so its only income is the one purchase. No recent figure exists for how many players of a free puzzle buy an unlock; old anchors run from 0.67% to 3.7%, and at 1% the sketch gives about $350 per 10,000 installs (Monetisation.md). | H | M | Make the free part good enough to earn trust and long enough to form a habit (tutorial and easy packs, complete). Put what the keenest players want (hard, expert) behind the purchase. Show the price plainly, before the gate. Price inside the range this audience already pays ($3.99–6.99) and lower in the markets where the book sold. Read the Play Console after launch: if few buy, test the price and the size of the free part before touching the model. Never answer low conversion with ads: that is ruled out. | STRAT-MIS-001, APP-PAY-001, APP-PAY-002 |
| R14 | **Billing mistakes.** Added 2026-11-25. A purchase that is not restored after a reinstall, a paid pack that will not open offline, or a gate that shows no price are each a refund and a one-star review. | M | H | One product, non-consumable; a visible restore action; the unlock remembered on the device; the store's localised price read and shown before the gate; manual test cases on a real device with a test account before release. Nothing is built yet. | APP-PAY-003, PROC-TST-002 |
