# STRAT-RSK-003 — Bloodless Theme, Truthful Rating

**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)

## Intent
A murder theme can raise the content rating, narrow ad inventory and collide with child-directed rules. Competitors sit at Everyone or Everyone 10+.

## Requirement
The murder theme stays cosy: no blood, gore, bodies or weapons in use, in art or text. The content-rating questionnaire is answered truthfully and its result recorded. The audience declaration, age handling and ad content settings are settled before ad code is written.
