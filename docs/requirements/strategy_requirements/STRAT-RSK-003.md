# STRAT-RSK-003 — Bloodless Theme, Truthful Rating

**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)

## Intent
A murder theme can raise the content rating and collide with child-directed rules. Competitors sit at Everyone or Everyone 10+. The game carries no ads (owner ruling 2026-11-25), so ad inventory and ad content settings no longer come into it.

## Requirement
The murder theme stays cosy: no blood, gore, bodies or weapons in use, in art or text. The content-rating questionnaire is answered truthfully and its result recorded. The audience declaration and age handling are settled before the purchase is built.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] A written tone rule exists and the art prompts and clue templates follow it
- [ ] The rating result and date are recorded
