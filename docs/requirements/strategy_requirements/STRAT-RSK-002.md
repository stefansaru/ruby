# STRAT-RSK-002 — Art Review Gate

**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)

## Intent
Two competitors already lose stars for art that looks machine-made, and our sheets are generated from prompts.

## Requirement
No art sheet enters the game until the owner has accepted it. Accepted sheets share one pixel style. Nothing false is said about how the art is made.

## Strategic Sources
- [RiskAnalysis.md](../../strategy/RiskAnalysis.md)
- [Art.md](../../Art.md)

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Every sheet in `sprites/` was accepted by the owner and has its prompt beside it
- [ ] Store and marketing copy make no claim of hand-drawn or AI-free art

Nothing built yet.
