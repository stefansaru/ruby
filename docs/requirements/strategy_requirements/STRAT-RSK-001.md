# STRAT-RSK-001 — Name and Trademark Check Before Listing

**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)

## Intent
'Detective Sudoku' is already the subtitle of a competing app. Finding out after launch costs the listing's reviews and installs.

## Requirement
Before the store listing is created, the title is searched on Google Play and the App Store and checked against trademark registers, and the owner decides whether to keep 'Detective Sudoku'.
