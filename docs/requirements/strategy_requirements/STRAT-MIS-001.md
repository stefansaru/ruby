# STRAT-MIS-001 — Free to Play, Fair Monetisation

**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)

## Intent
Intrusive ads are the genre's second-ranked complaint, and 43% of puzzle players say they quit over too many ads. A player gated from a logic puzzle leaves.

## Requirement
The game is free. In version 1 its only income is one passive banner and one rewarded video that the player chooses to watch for hint coins. There are no interstitials, no energy, no keys, no locked cases and no purchases.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] No purchase prompt or paywall exists in version 1
- [ ] No ad starts without a player action, other than the banner loading
- [ ] Every case in the game can be reached without watching an ad
- [ ] A rewarded ad that fails to load after the player agreed still pays out, within the stated bound
