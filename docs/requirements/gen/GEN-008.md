# GEN-008 — The victim gives no statement

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

No clue has the victim as its speaker.

## Why

The victim's square is found last, by elimination; that is the closing move of every case.

## How it is enforced

`parseCase` refuses such a clue; the generator never lists one.

- `test/engine/case_text_test.dart`
