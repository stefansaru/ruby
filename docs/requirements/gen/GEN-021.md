# GEN-021 — No statement gives the murderer away

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

No statement relates a suspect to the victim, and statements about company (alone, alone with, others, more) are never made about the victim's area.

## Why

"I was alone with Tom" where Tom is the victim is the answer, not a clue.

## How it is enforced

`listCandidates` in lib/gen/gen_clues.dart skips the victim and the victim's area.

- `test/gen/gen_test.dart`
- `test/data/packs_test.dart`
