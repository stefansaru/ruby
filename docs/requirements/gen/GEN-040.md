# GEN-040 — Play never breaks the board

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-25

## Requirement

Whatever a player does (any tap, any selection, hints taken or not, restarts), after every action: nobody is on a square that cannot hold a person and no square holds two; the clashes shown are exactly the people sharing a row or column; the crossed squares are exactly the free squares in a taken row or column; "solved" is shown exactly when the board is an answer for the oracle; marks on a full board are the oracle's truth; a hint names a wrong placement first and otherwise a true square, the victim last; and the real screen raises no error under random taps at several sizes.

## Why

A game is used in orders nobody wrote a test for. Fixed-sequence tests check the paths we thought of; a random player finds the others. "Solved exactly when the oracle agrees" also means no second answer can be reached by play.

## How it is enforced

A seeded random player in test/explore/play_test.dart checks the list after every action; a random tapper drives `CaseScreen` in test/explore/fuzz_test.dart.
