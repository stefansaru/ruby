# GEN-005 — Words about a square mean one thing

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

Beside means directly above, below, left or right and in the same area. In front of a door or window means on either side of it. A corner is two outline sides (grid) or two walls (area) that meet. Seeing runs straight along the row or column, stops at any object and at a wall without a door or window.

## Why

These are the words players argue about. Fixing them in code and in docs/GameRules.md lets the game show the squares a clue allows.

## How it is enforced

The `CellTest` classes in lib/engine/clue.dart.
