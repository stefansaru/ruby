# GEN-015 — A case's tier is the hardest technique it needs

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

Each tier accepts a range of techniques. A case belongs to a tier when the hardest technique the logic solver needs lies in that range; clue families are also limited by tier.

The ranges (2026-11-24, second version): tutorial and easy, clue to single (GEN-032); normal, last in line to pair; hard, subset to counting; expert, what if to liar split.

## Why

Size alone does not make a case hard. Grading by technique keeps "easy" easy and tells the hint system what to explain.

## How it is enforced

`Tier.accepts` and the tier gate in `generateCase`; `Family.since` in gen_clues.dart; the data test also shows the case is not solved without its hardest technique.
