# GEN-012 — No-touch means not even diagonally

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

With the no-touch rule no two people on the same grid stand on squares that touch, sideways or diagonally.

## Why

Borrowed from queens-style puzzles; it only works as a rule if the diagonal case is stated and checked.

## How it is enforced

`CaseDef.touching`, `CaseDef.check`, the no-touch pieces in `Atoms`.
