# GEN-032 — Tutorial and easy cases need no technique beyond clue and single

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

A tutorial or easy case is solved by reading each statement and by the rows and columns of people already placed: at every point some suspect has exactly one square left.

## Why

The first easy pack allowed "this row must hold someone", which ran through the victim's possible squares and could not be seen on the grid; the owner could not tell who to place next (2026-11-24, `input/problem2` to `problem4`). Easy means each step can be read off the grid.

## How it is enforced

`Tier.easy` in lib/engine/tier.dart (there since 2026-11-25) accepts clue and single only; the tier gate in `generateCase`; `checkShipped`.
