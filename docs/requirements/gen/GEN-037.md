# GEN-037 — A case made on request is as sound as a shipped one

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-25

## Requirement

`randomConfig(tier, seed)` picks a shape of the tier, a setting and a title from the seed; the case then passes every gate a shipped case passes and `checkShipped` for its tier. The same seed gives the same case. Its id begins with `random-`, so it is never mistaken for a shipped case.

## Why

The "Generate a random case" button under each pack (owner, 2026-11-25) must not be a way to meet a case with two answers or one that needs guessing.

## How it is enforced

`RandomCases.make` in lib/services/random_case.dart calls `generateCase`, the same function that builds the packs, then `checkShipped` on the text, and moves to the next seed when a seed yields nothing or fails the check. After 40 seeds it throws, and the list shows a message and frees the button (2026-11-25: the loop had no end and the check was not run).
