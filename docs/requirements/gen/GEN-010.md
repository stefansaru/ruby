# GEN-010 — A shipped case has exactly one solution

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

The exact solver finds every placement up to a limit. A shipped case has exactly one, and it is the certified one.

## Why

A second answer means a player can be right and be told they are wrong.

## How it is enforced

`solveExact`; the unique gate in `generateCase`; `checkShipped` reruns it on every stored case.
