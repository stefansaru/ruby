# GEN-023 — The engine decides, the view draws

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

lib/engine/ is pure Dart: no Flutter, no dart:io, no generator, no data. lib/gen/ has no Flutter and no dart:io, and nothing in the app imports it.

## Why

The rules must be testable without a device and reusable by tools; the generator must never ship inside the app.

## How it is enforced

test/architecture_test.dart reads the import lines of every source file.

## Covered by

- `test/architecture_test.dart`
