# GEN-024 — Wording is plain and exact

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

Each clue type has one fixed sentence. Columns are lettered and rows numbered; directions are above, below, left and right, never compass points; counts say "exactly".

## Why

The top-voted complaint about the market leader is "right column" meaning five different columns. Plain fixed sentences are the remedy.

## How it is enforced

`describeClue` in lib/engine/wording.dart.
