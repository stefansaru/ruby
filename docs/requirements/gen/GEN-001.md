# GEN-001 — One person per row and column on each grid

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

On each grid no two people share a row and no two share a column. With two grids the rule holds on each grid separately.

## Why

It is the rule the whole genre rests on; every deduction about blocking a row or column follows from it.

## How it is enforced

`CaseDef.conflict` is the one definition. `CaseDef.check`, both solvers and the scene drawer all call it.
