# GEN-038 — The rules have a second witness

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-25

## Requirement

`test/support/oracle.dart` states the placement rules and the meaning of every clue a second time, written from docs/GameRules.md and reading the case text itself. It imports nothing from the game. The engine must agree with it: on every clue of every shipped case on random boards (right and wrong); on whether a board is an answer; on every clue form the notation allows, including forms the generator does not draw; and the exact solver must find exactly the answers the oracle finds by trying every board, on scenes small enough to try them all.

## Why

Everything in the engine derives from one meaning of a clue (GEN-004). That keeps the parts consistent with each other, and it also means one misread rule is wrong everywhere at once and no test built on the engine can see it. A second reading of the rules, sharing no code, can (2026-11-25, owner: "ensure that no logical flaws get overseen").

## How it is enforced

`test/architecture_test.dart` fails if the oracle or the mirror helper imports the game. The comparisons are in test/explore/.

## Covered by

- `test/explore/meaning_test.dart`
- `test/explore/solvers_test.dart`
- `test/architecture_test.dart`
