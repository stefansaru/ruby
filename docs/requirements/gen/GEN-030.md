# GEN-030 — The victim is listed last and begins with V

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

In every shipped case the victim is the last person of the cast, has the letter V and a name beginning with V. No suspect has the letter V.

## Why

The disc on the grid shows one letter. "V" reads as "victim" at a glance; a victim called Will with a "W" looked like one more suspect (owner report, 2026-11-24).

## How it is enforced

`_victims` in lib/gen/gen_scene.dart; `checkShipped` refuses any other victim.

- `test/data/mapping_test.dart`
