# GEN-017 — A config that makes no sense is refused by field

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

`GenConfig` throws an `ArgumentError` naming the field for an unknown setting, a grid count other than one or two, sides outside 3 to 12, a cast outside 3 to 12, no attempts, or a liar case outside the expert tier.

## Why

A slot that can never be filled should fail when it is written, with the field named, not after four hundred draws.

## How it is enforced

The `GenConfig` constructor.
