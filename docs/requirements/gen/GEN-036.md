# GEN-036 — A statement's mark follows the placements

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-25

## Requirement

`CaseSession.markOf` gives each statement one of three marks from the people placed so far: open, met or broken. A statement is open while its speaker is not placed and while it depends on someone not yet placed; it is broken as soon as the people placed contradict it; statements that count people wait until everyone is placed. On a finished placement the mark equals `Clue.holds`. Correct placements never show a broken mark, except the one false statement of a liar case.

The pieces of a statement are made once per session and without the case rules
(`Atoms(..., rules: false)`), so a mark is about that statement alone (2026-11-25).

## Why

The statements are shown as a checklist with a green tick or a red cross (owner, 2026-11-25). A mark that disagreed with the rules would teach the player something false, so the mark is computed in the engine from the same clue pieces the solvers use.

## How it is enforced

`markOf` and `victimMark` in lib/engine/session.dart, built on `Atoms`.
