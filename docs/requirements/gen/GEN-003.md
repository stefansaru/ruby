# GEN-003 — The murderer is the one other person in the victim's area

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

In a valid placement the victim's area holds exactly two people: the victim and the murderer. A placement where the victim is alone, or has two or more companions, is not an answer.

## Why

This is how the case is won; it also removes placements, so both solvers must apply it as a rule and not only read it off at the end.

## How it is enforced

`CaseDef.murdererIn` and `CaseDef.check`; the victim rule piece in `Atoms` (`victimRule`), used by both solvers.
