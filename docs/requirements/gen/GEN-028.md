# GEN-028 — Packs are well formed as a set

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

Slot ids, seeds and titles are unique across all packs, and every pack visits every setting. The test checks the packs after the tutorial; the tutorial has held all twelve settings since it grew to 100 slots (2026-11-25).

## Why

Ids are how progress is saved; a repeated title or a pack stuck in one place reads as careless.

## How it is enforced

`campaign` in lib/gen/gen_campaign.dart; `titleFor` gives a different title per index.

## Covered by

- `test/gen/gen_test.dart`
