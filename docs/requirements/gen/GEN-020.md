# GEN-020 — Every suspect says something

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

Every suspect has at least one statement.

## Why

Each suspect is a card on screen; a card with nothing on it reads as a bug.

## How it is enforced

`chooseClues` gives everyone a first statement and never drops a last one; `checkShipped`.
