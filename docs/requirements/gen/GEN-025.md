# GEN-025 — Clues in a case are a mix

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

From the normal tier up a case uses at least three clue families, two below that, and no family makes up more than six tenths of its clues.

## Why

Ten "beside a tree" sentences solve nothing new and read as machine output.

## How it is enforced

The variety gate in `generateCase`.
