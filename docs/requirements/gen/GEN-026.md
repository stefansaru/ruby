# GEN-026 — The solver explains itself and accepts the player's placements

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

`solveLogic` takes squares the player has already decided. From any correct partial placement it still solves the case; from a wrong one it reports a contradiction. Every step records its technique, the person, the clue and the squares it placed or ruled out.

Since 2026-11-25: a hint's square is the square the solver placed, not a look-up of the
stored answer, and is worked out once per board. In a liar case `LogicResult.lie` names
the false statement, and a hint says "The false statement is X's" instead of quoting it
as something X said. With two grids a square, a row or a column is named with its grid.

## Why

This is what a hint is made of: the next step from where the player is, with its reason.

## How it is enforced

The `fixed` argument of `solveLogic`; `LogicStep`.
