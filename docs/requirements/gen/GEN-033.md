# GEN-033 — A title belongs to its setting

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-25

## Requirement

A case title is "The", an adjective and a word from the title list of the case's own setting (`SettingDef.titles`). No word is in two settings' lists, and no title is used twice across the shipped packs. A case made on request draws from title indexes the packs do not use.

## Why

Titles were drawn from one list for every place, so a train case could be "The Muddy Matchbox" (owner report, 2026-11-25, `input/problem5`). A title is the first thing read; it should say where you are.

## How it is enforced

`titleFor(setting, index)` in lib/gen/gen_scene.dart; the per-setting title index in `campaign` and `randomConfig` (lib/gen/gen_campaign.dart).
