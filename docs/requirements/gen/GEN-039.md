# GEN-039 — A case seen in a mirror is the same puzzle

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-25

## Requirement

Mirrored left to right, or top to bottom, a shipped case still parses, still has exactly one answer (the mirror of the original), has the same murderer and is still solved by logic. With its extra statements counted as clues a case keeps its one answer and is no harder.

## Why

A rule that confuses left with right, above with below, rows with columns or the two sides of a door is consistent with itself and so passes every ordinary test. It cannot pass both mirrors.

## How it is enforced

`mirrored` in test/support/transform.dart turns the case text over; the tests solve the result.

## Covered by

- `test/explore/symmetry_test.dart`
