# GEN-041 — The sentence says what the clue means

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-25

## Requirement

For every statement and general clue of every shipped case, the English sentence names what the clue names (area, object, row number, column letter, other person), uses above/below and left/right as the numbers say, states the clue's counts, denies exactly when the clue is a denial, uses he/his for a man and she/her for a woman, and keeps the order of "more in A than in B".

## Why

The engine can check a clue correctly while the sentence tells the player the opposite. No other test reads the English. Wording is the most reported fault in this genre (docs/strategy/PainPointsAndConcepts.md).

## How it is enforced

`faults` in test/explore/words_test.dart derives the words a sentence must and must not contain from the notation in the case text, without using the engine's wording; a second test feeds it wrong sentences to prove it notices.

## Covered by

- `test/explore/words_test.dart`
