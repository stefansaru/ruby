# GEN-006 — The parser is the validator

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-24

## Requirement

A case that parses is well formed: every name resolves, the grid is complete, every area is one connected piece on one grid, ids are unique, there is exactly one victim, the cast fits the grids, doors and windows sit on walls, and impossible relations are refused.

## Why

A malformed case must fail a test, not a phone. Having no way to build a broken `CaseDef` from text means nothing downstream needs to defend against one.

## How it is enforced

`parseCase` in lib/engine/case_text.dart throws `CaseFormatException` with the line number.

## Covered by

- `test/engine/case_text_test.dart`
