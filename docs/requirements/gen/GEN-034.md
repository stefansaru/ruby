# GEN-034 — No door or window unless a clue uses it

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-25

## Requirement

A shipped case shows doors only when a clue says "in front of a door", windows only when a clue says "in front of a window", and both when a clue is about what someone can see (openings let people see through walls). Tutorial and easy cases have none.

## Why

Doors and windows that no clue mentions are noise, and they sit on the walls where the area names are now printed (owner, 2026-11-25).

## How it is enforced

`usedOpenings` in lib/engine/openings.dart (called by `generateCase`) drops the rest before the uniqueness check; `checkShipped` refuses a stored case with an unused opening.
