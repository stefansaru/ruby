# GEN-035 — Extra statements are true and never needed

**Status:** enforced
**Since:** 2026-11-25

## Requirement

A case may carry extra statements (`extra` lines, `CaseDef.extras`), each spoken by a suspect. Every extra is true in the certified solution, repeats no other statement, and is never a statement about company made from the victim's area. The solvers, the rules and the difficulty grade never read them: the case is the same puzzle without them. The generator tops each suspect up to three statements in all, where the scene has that many true things to say.

A liar case has no extras (2026-11-25). *Why:* a true extra beside the lie would name
the liar, and leaving only the liar without extras would too.

## Why

Players asked for more to go on in easier play (owner, 2026-11-25): a "+" on a card reveals one more statement. Keeping extras apart from the needed clues lets the same case be played with more or less help, and keeps "no statement can be left out" (GEN-019) true of the clues.

## How it is enforced

`parseCase` refuses a false extra, an extra with no speaker and an extra by the victim; `chooseExtras` in lib/gen/gen_clues.dart draws them from the same list of true statements as the clues, after the case is accepted; `checkShipped` refuses a repeated statement.
