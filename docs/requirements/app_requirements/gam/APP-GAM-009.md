# APP-GAM-009 — Case Selection Without Gates

**Tags:** Gameplay, Progression
**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)
**NORM Sources:** (derived; no normative register exists yet)

> The exact unlock rule (strict order, or a few cases ahead) is the agent's proposal and waits for the owner.

## Requirement
Cases are grouped by setting and tier. A case opens when the one before it in its pack is solved; the first case of every pack in the easy tier is open from the start. Nothing else locks a case: no coins, ads, keys, timers or purchases.

## Why
Order gives a learning curve. Any other lock is a gate, and gates are a quoted reason for leaving a competitor.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] The unlock rule is shown to the player
- [ ] No case requires an ad or coins
- [ ] Progress is stored on the device and survives restart
