# APP-GAM-011 — Mistakes Are Not Punished

**Tags:** Gameplay, Tone
**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)
**NORM Sources:** (derived; no normative register exists yet)

## Requirement
The game has no lives, no mistake counter that ends a case and no timer that fails it. A wrong placement stays on the board until the player or a hint finds it.

## Why
The audience plays for calm. Punishing a trial placement punishes the way a deduction puzzle is solved.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] No fail state exists in a case
- [ ] If time or hint use is shown at the end, it is information, not a score that locks anything
