# APP-GAM-003 — Notes per Suspect

**Tags:** Gameplay, Notes
**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)
**NORM Sources:** (derived; no normative register exists yet)

## Requirement
The player can mark, for each person separately, squares where that person might be and squares where they cannot be, and can cross a square for everyone. A long press on an area crosses it for the selected person.

## Why
The deductions the game asks for are about one person at a time. A reviewer of the market leader found 'no way to make a note of that'.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] There is one notes layer per person and one for everyone
- [ ] Marks of the other layers stay faintly visible
- [ ] An area can be crossed for one person in one gesture
- [ ] Notes are saved with the case
