# APP-UX-005 — A Line of Story

**Tags:** UX, Tone, Content
**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)
**NORM Sources:** (derived; no normative register exists yet)

## Requirement
A case opens with a title and one or two lines that set the scene, and ends with a line naming the murderer, the victim and the area. The tone is cosy and bloodless.

## Why
A fan of the market leader asks for case intros. A sentence is enough to make a grid a whodunit.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Every case has a title
- [ ] Intro and ending text come from templates and the case's own data
- [ ] No text describes violence
