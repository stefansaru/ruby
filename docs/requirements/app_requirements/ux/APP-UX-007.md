# APP-UX-007 — A Clue List You Can Work With

**Tags:** UX, Gameplay
**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)
**NORM Sources:** (derived; no normative register exists yet)

## Requirement
The clue list shows each person with their clue, lets the player tick a clue as used, dims the clues of placed people, and keeps case-wide clues in their own group at the top.

## Why
The book's own advice for a stuck solver is to re-read every clue, including general ones. The list should make that quick.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Case-wide clues are visibly separate
- [ ] Ticking a clue is the player's own mark and is saved
- [ ] Tapping a person's clue selects that person
