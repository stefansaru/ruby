# APP-PRV-003 — Privacy Policy and Age Handling

**Tags:** Privacy, Legal, Release
**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)
**NORM Sources:** (derived; no normative register exists yet)

> The policy page is part of the website work, which is a TODO item by owner decision of 2026-11-24.

## Requirement
A privacy policy that describes the app truthfully is published and linked from the app and the listing. The declared audience and the handling of younger players (including what a purchase by a child means under store policy) are decided and recorded before the purchase is built. The app carries no ads (owner ruling 2026-11-25), so there are no ad settings to decide.

## Why
A murder theme and an in-app purchase both touch store policy; the family persona means the question cannot be skipped.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] The policy exists at a public address and matches the build
- [ ] The audience decision and its date are recorded
- [ ] The Play data-safety form matches the policy
