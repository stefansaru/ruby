# APP-ACC-001 — Nothing by Colour Alone

**Tags:** Accessibility, Grid
**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)
**NORM Sources:** (derived; no normative register exists yet)

## Requirement
People are told apart by a letter on their token as well as its colour. Areas are told apart by their border and name as well as their floor. Crosses, candidates and conflicts differ in shape, not only in colour.

## Why
Roughly one man in twelve has a colour-vision difference, and coloured-room grids are the genre's default look.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Each token carries its person's initial
- [ ] A greyscale screenshot of a case is still playable
