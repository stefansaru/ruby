# APP-ACC-003 — Screen Reader Labels

**Tags:** Accessibility
**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)
**NORM Sources:** (derived; no normative register exists yet)

## Requirement
Every square has a spoken label with its coordinates, its area, its object and who is on it. Every clue is read as its text. Controls are labelled.

## Why
The game is text and positions; nothing about it needs sight in principle.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] A case can be read through with TalkBack
- [ ] Labels come from the same templates as the visible text
