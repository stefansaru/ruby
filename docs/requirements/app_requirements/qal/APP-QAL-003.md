# APP-QAL-003 — Plays Offline

**Tags:** Quality, Platform
**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)
**NORM Sources:** (derived; no normative register exists yet)

## Requirement
Every case, note, hint and setting works with no network. Without a network, the banner is absent and rewarded ads cannot be offered; nothing else changes and nothing waits on a request.

## Why
Competitors list offline play as a feature, and a puzzle is played on trains.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] A manual test in aeroplane mode covers a full case with a paid hint
- [ ] No screen blocks on a network call
