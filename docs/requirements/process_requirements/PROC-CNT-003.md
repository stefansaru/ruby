# PROC-CNT-003 — Player-Facing Words Are Proposed First

**Tags:** Process, Content, UX
**Status:** ❌ Pending (written 2026-11-24)

> The English clue templates written in round 1 are first drafts under this rule and wait for the owner's review.

## Requirement
A change to text the player reads (clue templates, hint text, glossary, UI strings, store copy) is shown to the owner as before and after and waits for approval before it is committed. First drafts of new text are marked as drafts until approved.

## Why
Wording is the product. pushy and amethyst both hold the same rule after copy changed under the owner's feet.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] A commit that changes player-facing text cites the approval
- [ ] Unapproved text is marked draft
