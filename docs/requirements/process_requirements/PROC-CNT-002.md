# PROC-CNT-002 — No Third-Party Content

**Tags:** Process, Content, Legal
**Status:** ⚠️ Partial (written 2026-11-24)

> Partial: a rule followed by review, not checked by a tool.

## Requirement
Names of people, areas, objects and settings, case titles, story lines and clue wording are written for this game. Nothing is transcribed from the source books or from another app. Research docs may quote sources briefly, with attribution, and are not shipped in the app.

## Why
See STRAT-VAL-003: mechanics are common ground, expression is not.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Cast names come from the game's own name lists
- [ ] No test fixture reproduces a published puzzle
- [ ] Research quotations are short and attributed

## Implementation
In force for everything written on 2026-11-24.
