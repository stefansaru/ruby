# Reel Scripts — draft

**Draft 1, 2026-11-24.** Building blocks for 20–40 second vertical videos:
pick one hook, one middle, one ending. All need a playable build to record;
none can be filmed yet. Screen text stays within the truth rules of
[Campaign_Launch.md](Campaign_Launch.md).

## Hooks (first two seconds)

1. "Five people. One of them did it. You have four clues."
2. "It's sudoku, but somebody's been murdered."
3. "Nobody shares a row. Nobody shares a column. Somebody shares a room with the victim."
4. "Can you find who was alone with the victim?"
5. "This puzzle has exactly one answer. Find it."
6. "I thought this clue was vague. Then I tapped it."
7. "Dana was beside a tree. There are three trees. Watch."
8. "The clue says column C. Not 'the right column'. Column C."
9. "No guessing. If you're guessing, you missed something."
10. "One row left. One square free. Somebody has to be there."
11. "This case took me six minutes. Here's the step I missed."
12. "The victim goes in the last square. So who else is in that room?"
13. "You don't need to know who's in this square yet. Just that someone is."
14. "Two chairs. Ben is on one of them. That already rules out a square."
15. "A whodunit you can finish before the kettle boils."
16. "Same rules, new place: today we're on a train."

## Middles

**M1. The full solve (easy case, sped up).** Show the grid and clues. Place
the person with only one possible square. Cross the row and column. Repeat.
Slow down for the last placement. On-screen text names each step in three or
four words.

**M2. One technique.** Freeze on a stuck board. Ask "what now?". Reveal one
technique (line with one free square; intersecting squares; counting an area).
Show it unlock the rest.

**M3. The clue preview.** Read a clue aloud. Ask the viewer which squares it
allows. Tap the clue; the squares light up. Good for the clarity message.

**M4. Notes.** "Dana wasn't in the Kitchen." One long press crosses the Kitchen
for Dana only. Then show how that leaves her one square.

**M5. The hint that teaches.** Stuck board. Ask for the explanation. Read the
sentence the game gives. Place. *(Only once the hint ladder exists.)*

## Endings

**E1.** "Who was alone with the victim? Answer in the comments." (Cut before
the reveal. Never on a daily case on its day.)

**E2.** The reveal: the murderer's token, the room outlined, one line: "Ben was
alone with Vera in the Garden."

**E3.** "Detective Sudoku. Free. Every case has one answer." Store badge.

## Rules for every clip

- Show the real app, at real speed or honestly sped up.
- Never show a daily case's answer on its day.
- No blood, no weapons, no bodies, in picture or text. The tone is cosy.
- Captions on; many viewers watch muted.
- Names in clips are the game's own generated cast, never a book's.
